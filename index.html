Modular software development for PC and mobile

Software Developer

A modular software system developed to track truck movements between quarries and construction sites, as well as fuel distribution across fixed and mobile stations.

The solution combined desktop applications for different control points with Android applications used during material reception and mobile fuel delivery.

Desktop modules

  • Vehicle registration with carrier, driver, truck and load capacity information.
  • Departure registration with QR scanning.
  • Weighing station registration with gross and net weight data.
  • Fixed-station fuel dispatch with printed tickets and QR-coded records.
  • Record search with filters and CSV export.
  • User and password management.

Android modules

  • Receiving registration at the quarry or weighing station using QR scanning and GPS location.
  • Mobile-station fuel dispatch registration with cloud backup.
  • Offline mode with synchronization of pending records after reconnecting.
